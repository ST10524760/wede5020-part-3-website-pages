About Soweto Global Solutions

Empowering regional workplaces with accessible IT support, software mastery, and standardized business processes.

Our Story

Founded in Soweto, Gauteng, Soweto Global Solutions (SGS) was established to address the growing need for practical, localized technology solutions. We specialize in providing comprehensive IT support, hands-on Microsoft Office training, and clear workplace documentation (SOPs) tailored for businesses and organizations across the region.

Our team brings together tech expertise and community commitment, helping teams work smarter, reduce operational errors, and bridge key technology skill gaps.
